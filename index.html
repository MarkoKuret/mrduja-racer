Plan the race

Routes your Bavaria through 8 forecast models and shows which side of the channel they favour.

How to read the map

Each coloured line is the fastest route for one model. Solid lines go to Mrduja and dashed lines come back. The dots mark every 30 minutes. Drag the time slider to see where each model puts you and what the wind is doing. If the high-resolution models (ICON-2I and the two HARMONIEs) agree on a side, bet on it. If they split, stay in the middle and go to whoever shows the new breeze first.

1. Pin the start line

Go right next to each end and hold the phone at the bow. It averages 10 seconds of GPS.

2. Measure the wind (optional)

Using the forecast wind until you measure

Sail close-hauled on each tack for 10 seconds and record both. That's the most accurate way. A measured wind changes the line bias, and replaces the forecast near the line for the first 45 minutes of the plan.
